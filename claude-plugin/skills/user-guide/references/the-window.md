# Find your way around

## Pages

The sidebar lists the pages. Press `g` and a page's number to jump to it.

| Page | Key | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `g1` | Your day: what finished, what waits on you, what is next |
| Tasks | `g2` | Everything you want done, in your order |
| Git | `g3` | Pull requests, their checks and what is ready to merge |
| Agents & jobs | `g4` | Agents working now, scheduled jobs and past runs |
| Inbox | `g5` | Questions and reviews waiting for you |
| Environment | `g7` | What agents see in each repository: instructions, hooks, CI, variables |
| Library | `g8` | Your project's docs, and the files agents read |
| Settings | `g9` | Look, keys and connections |
| Analysis | `g0` | Charts of your tasks and your usage |

Hide pages you don't use in Settings › Pages. `⌘[` and `⌘]` go back and
forward. `⌃⌘S` hides the sidebar.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a named group of folders you work on together, usually Git
repositories; a folder you have not grouped is a workspace of its own. The
picker at the top of a page chooses which workspaces it shows: one, several or
all.
Click a workspace's square to give it a colour; the colour follows it on
panes and tasks. Add workspaces in Settings › Workspaces.

## The composer

The box at the bottom of every page is where you ask for work. `⌘L` puts the
cursor in it from anywhere. See [Agents](agents.md#ask-for-work).

## Panes

Tools open beside the page as panes: a task's **Detail**, its **Agent**, the
**Inbox** and others. Open one from the icons in the title bar. A pane
follows the task you select; `⌥⌘P` locks it to the one it shows.

| Key | Pane action |
|---|---|
| `⌥⌘D` | Split the pane, then choose what the new one shows |
| `⌥⌘F` | Flip the pane to its settings, and back |
| `⌥⌘Z` | Zoom the pane to fill the window, and back |
| `⌥⌘W` | Close the pane |
| `⌥⌘` arrow | Move the keyboard to the pane that way |

Drag the line between two panes to resize them. Double-click it to even
them out.

## Right-click anything

Every row, cell, header, pane and button has a menu of what you can do to it,
with each action's key beside it. `⌥⌘M` opens the same menu for whatever has
the keyboard.

## Every key

`?` lists every key. Change any of them in Settings › Keys.
